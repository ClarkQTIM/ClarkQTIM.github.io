## Overview

Annealed Quantum Feature Maps (AQFM) investigate how
time-dependent quantum Hamiltonians can generate feature
representations for classical machine learning.

The project studies how annealing timescales, measurement
observables, and quantum interactions influence the
information captured by these representations.
